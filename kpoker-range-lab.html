About these ranges

K Poker plays 7-handed with an ante from every seat and a small blind as big as the big blind, so the pot starts at 9bb before anyone acts. Limping is common and opens are big. These charts come from a preflop solver run on exactly those rules.

  1. Start from the hand

    At the table you see your cards first. Hand lens shows one hand in every common spot: first in from each seat, against limpers, against an open, after you limped and someone raised, and in 3-bet and 4-bet pots.

  2. Each square is the most common line

    Many lines lead to the same seat matchup (who limped before the raise, who called after). Each lens square shows the line that comes up most often; tap it to open that exact spot in Charts, where every other line for the seat is listed with how often it comes up.

  3. Multiway pots are estimates

    When two or more players are already in, the solver values the flop with a learned estimate rather than solving it out. Those spots carry a small corner mark: learn the shape, but trust them less than heads-up spots.

  4. Frequencies only

    The solver gives how often each hand takes each action, not what each choice is worth. A hand split between two actions is close to indifferent between them.
