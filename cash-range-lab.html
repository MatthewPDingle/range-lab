How to learn cash ranges

Cash ranges hinge on seats far more than on stack depth. So learn them as one picture per spot type: which seat a hand starts playing from, and how far each ladder reaches. Then learn how deeper stacks, bigger opens and a straddle move the edges.

  1. One seat map instead of seven charts

    Charts → First in → Seat map shows, for every hand, the earliest seat it opens from. KTo reads “HJ”: open it from the HJ and later. One chart covers the whole opening game. The same view for BB defence tells you the earliest opener you defend each hand against.

  2. Learn the edges

    Each range is a few ladders (pairs, Ax suited, Kx offsuit…). Memorise where each ladder stops in each seat. The Edges panel and the Edges drill train exactly that.

  3. File it under the hand

    At the table you see your cards first. Hand lens shows one hand across every opener-versus-you matchup, so “A5s” becomes a small picture: open everywhere, 3-bet against late opens, call against early ones.

  4. Learn the adjustments as changes

    Start at 100bb with 3x opens. Then turn on Changes and slide to deeper stacks, bigger opens, or the straddle game. Only the ringed hands are new. That's how to handle live 5x opens without learning new charts from scratch.

  5. Recall beats re-reading

    Paint ranges from memory and drill fast decisions on edge hands. Misses come back on a spaced schedule.

Suggested order

Step 1First in at 100bb: the Seat map, then Paint each seat. Quick-fire “First in”.
Step 2Big blind against each opener: call or 3-bet. Seats drill on BB defence.
Step 3Facing an open from other seats: flat or 3-bet. Hand lens on suited aces and broadway hands.
Step 4Facing a 3-bet: in position and out of position. Edges drill.
Step 54-bets and squeezes.
Step 6Adjustments: deeper stacks, bigger opens, the straddle game. Changes mode on each.
ThenMixed Quick-fire daily, red squares in “Where you leak” first.

Every session: 10 minutes of Paint, Edges or Seats, then 15 minutes of Quick-fire. Reviews that are due come first automatically.

After a session

Rebuild the hands you weren't sure about in Spot builder: tap the seats in the order they acted, then slide the stack. Do it on a break or after play; live events don't allow checking charts during a hand.

About the numbers

Solutions: 8-handed cash with cold calls. Regular game: 10% live rake, opens from 2x to 7x at 100–300bb. Straddle game: a 2bb straddle with the straddler acting last, NL25 rake (the heaviest rake offered for straddle games), opens from 4bb to 7bb at 50–1,600bb. These are equilibrium ranges. A live table that over-calls or rarely 3-bets is worth adjusting against, but learn the baseline first.
